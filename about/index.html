Our Story

Made the Way We Were Taught

Mittaikaran began with one idea — that a sweet should taste of what it is made from. Not of sugar alone, and not of ghee poured in to hide a shortcut.

A Counter, Not a Factory

Everything we sell is made in small batches, in our own kitchen in Coimbatore, and it is made every morning. Nothing waits in storage for a festival to arrive.

That limits how much we can make in a day. It also means the burfi you buy on Tuesday was not made the Thursday before.

◆Balance is not a recipe. It is a habit.

From Kitchen to Counter

Four things happen to every sweet before it reaches your hands.

  1. 01

    Sourcing

    Nuts, ghee and jaggery are bought at the grade a premium counter would use. This is the part we refuse to economise on.

  2. 02

    The Kitchen

    Small batches, traditional method, made fresh the same morning. No preservatives go in, because nothing needs to last that long.

  3. 03

    The Balance

    Sugar enough to sweeten, ghee enough to finish. Neither is allowed to take over the ingredient underneath.

  4. 04

    The Counter

    Priced for an ordinary week, not only for a festival. The quality does not move with the occasion.
